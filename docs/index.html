Before

Psychology, Social Work and Psychopedagogy kept separate paper trails and disconnected systems. A high-risk signal raised in one office could sit unseen by the others for days — precisely when speed matters most.

With CareUCE

A shared, event-driven backbone propagates case updates the instant they happen. Departments keep their own confidential records, but no one operates blind to an active crisis.
